Why achievements are worth planning for

Achievements in UntoldLife are more than badges. Each one you earn adds 1% to all the gold and all the experience you get, and that bonus belongs to the family, so it never resets when you retire. The full set of 54 is worth +54% on everything you earn. The counters behind most of them, such as crops harvested or rocks broken, keep adding up across generations, so every life moves you forward even if you retire before a goal is reached.

Most achievements arrive simply by playing. A few depend on a generation that has not opened yet, and a few take a real decision. Knowing which is which lets you stop worrying about the first group and spend your attention on the second.

The ones that come by themselves

These need no special plan. Keep playing the way the game guides you and they fall into place:

  • The first rung of each counter. Harvest 100 crops, collect 100 animal products, break 100 rocks, defeat 100 monsters and complete 10 quests. All of these usually happen in the first generation.
  • Millionaire. The first three generations ask you to earn 1 million gold before you can retire, so this one comes with your first retirement.
  • Dungeon diver (floor 10) and Second generation. Retiring also needs dungeon floor 30, which takes you past floor 10 on the way.
  • The second rung of the counters. 1,000 crops, products, rocks and monsters, and 100 quests, come within the next few generations, faster once helpers do the work.
  • Adventurer (floor 50). After the third generation the retirement goal climbs three dungeon floors per generation, so by around generation 10 you pass floor 50 just to retire.
  • Village tycoon (100 million gold across generations). The gold goal for retiring grows from the fourth generation on, and the totals of those goals alone reach about 100 million near the tenth generation.

A small tip: the weekly challenge in the «This week» tab counts the same things, harvests, animal products, fish, rocks, monsters and quests. Working on the weekly challenge moves these achievements forward too, and pays +10% gold and experience for seven days on top.

The ones that wait for a generation

Some achievements cannot be started early, because the thing they count is locked:

  • Angler and Lord of the lake (100 and 1,000 fish). The lake opens in the second generation. Hire fishers there and the count rises while you do other things.
  • Old bloodline and Legendary family (generations 5 and 10). They mark your progress through the legacy path.
  • The valley's wedding. Marriage opens in the fifth generation. You need 10 hearts with a villager who can marry, and 50,000 gold for the proposal.
  • Challenger. Challenge generations also open in the fifth generation, chosen when you retire.
  • Child of the depths (dungeon floor 200). Floors past 100, the Forgotten Depths, open in the fourth generation.
  • Dawn of industry and every factory achievement. The factory needs generation 10, 80% of the legacy tree's levels and 1,000 legacy stars.

The ones that need a plan

Friend of the village

Ten hearts with one person. Give that person a gift they like every day, and watch the season, because what they like changes. Someone who gets nothing for half a season gets upset and loses affection, so it is better to pick one or two people and be steady with them than to spread gifts thin. The perk «Grandfather's name» starts every villager at 3.5 hearts, and the heirloom «Grandmother's ring» adds half a heart per level.

Deep floors in the mine

Mine floors 50, 100 and 200 depend on the pickaxe. Each pickaxe level has a floor limit (12, 28, 48, 75, 105 and 150), and only the seventh level has none, so floor 200 needs a top pickaxe. The keystone perk «Dynamite» makes every cleared floor take you down two, which halves the climb.

Deep floors in the dungeon

Floor 100 and floor 200 are about gear and food more than time. The perk «Battle memory» starts each generation at part of your family's deepest floor, up to 50%, so you do not climb the early floors again every life.

The challenge achievements

Unbeatable and Master of challenges need 10 and 30 completed challenges. You can run only one challenge per generation, but the six challenges can be repeated, and each completion raises its permanent reward. Thirty means many generations, so start taking challenges as soon as they open and pick ones that fit your strongest side: «Warrior only» if your fighter is strong, «Underground» if your mine is.

The immortals and Emperor of the valley

Generation 25 and 10 billion gold are the long goals of the village half. They come with time, and the factory's public services and train help the gold one.

A suggested order

  1. Generation 1: the first rung of every counter, Millionaire, Dungeon diver. Retire as soon as you can.
  2. Generations 2 to 4: Second generation, Angler, the 1,000 counters, Prospector (mine floor 50). Start giving gifts to one villager every day.
  3. Generation 5: Old bloodline, the wedding, Friend of the village, and your first challenge.
  4. Generations 6 to 10: one challenge per life, Adventurer, Lord of the lake, Village tycoon, Legendary family, and the deeper mine and dungeon floors.
  5. The factory: Dawn of industry, then contracts, the town hall, the rails and the boat. Renewing the factory opens once the town hall reaches level 10, and the port opens with the second stage of the train.
  6. The long run: The immortals, Master of challenges, the deepest floors, Emperor of the valley, the complete invention map and the First Train.

For the full rules of each system, see Generations and legacy, The mine and the smith and The factory.
