Animals and fishing

The barn gives you income that does not care about the season. Every animal makes one product on a fixed timer, and a happy animal makes more. You unlock new animals by raising your ranching level, which grows as you collect products.

Fishing

Fishing opens in the second generation, with three waters that open as your fishing level rises: 8 fish in the lake, 5 in the river and 5 in the sea, from common ones to rare catches worth thousands.

Compare that with the field: a turnip plot makes about 21 gold a minute and a strawberry plot about 60. In the first generation the barn is the fastest way to grow your income, which is why the barn opens as soon as you hire your first harvester.

Happiness is a speed dial

An animal's speed is 0.5 plus its happiness. A fully happy animal works at ×1.5, and a completely sad one at ×0.5. So a sad barn makes only a third of what a happy barn makes. Nothing else in the barn matters as much.

Happiness falls by itself. From full to empty takes about 14 minutes, and it keeps falling while the game is closed. Each tap on «Pet» adds 25 happiness to that kind of animal. The rancher (6,000 gold, in the village) keeps every animal at full happiness all the time, at home or away. If you plan to close the game for hours, the rancher is the best 6,000 gold you can spend.

Filling the barn the smart way

Each animal costs 25% more than the last one of the same kind. Six chickens cost 150, 188, 234, 293, 366 and 458, which is 1,689 in total. The sixth chicken costs 458 and makes 150 a minute, so it pays back in about three minutes. The first cow costs 1,500 and makes 360 a minute, so it pays back in about four. That is why it makes sense to fill the cheaper animals first, then move up.

Room is the other limit. At the start the barn holds 6 of each kind. Each barn expansion adds about five more spots per kind: 10, 16, 20, 26, 30 and finally 36. The number is always even, so every animal can find a breeding partner. The expansions cost 1,200, 3,600, 10,800, 32,400, 97,200 and 291,600.

The shepherd's crook at the smith adds 50% production speed per tier. The first tier costs 500 gold and 20 copper, the same as any first tool upgrade. If the barn is your main income, it is worth more than a better hoe at that point. Each ranching level adds another 2%.

New animals open with your ranching level: cow at 3, sheep at 5, goat at 6, beehive at 8, turkey at 10 and camel at 12. Ranching XP is one eighth of what each product is worth, so the pricier animals also level you faster.

Breeding: premium and golden animals

Put two animals of the same kind and grade in a breeding house. If it works, both move up a grade. If it fails, you lose both animals.

  • Premium (ranching level 5): makes two products instead of one.
  • Golden (ranching level 10, bred from two premium animals): makes two golden products, each worth five times the plain one. A golden animal earns ten times a plain one.

The base chances are 20% for premium and 5% for golden. «Breeding tools» in the village raise them to 25% and 8% (25,000 gold), then 33% and 12% (300,000 gold), and shorten the house's rest from 5 minutes to 4, then 2. Salma's hearts raise the chances a lot more: about 10% more per heart, so at 10 hearts the best tools reach about 67% and 25%.

Practise on chickens. Losing two chickens costs a few hundred gold. Losing two camels costs more than a million. Breed the cheap animals while the chances are low, and only try the expensive ones once Salma likes you and the tools are upgraded. Extra breeding houses cost 25,000, then five times more each. With all six, you can set one animal to breed by itself.

Gifts and guild deliveries that ask for milk also accept golden milk, so a barn that turned golden can still fill them.

Fishing, from the second generation

The lake tab opens in the second generation. Each tap on «Cast!» adds your fishing power, and a fish bites when the power reaches 6 plus half your fishing level. At level 1 that is about seven taps per fish. You can also hold the button down: it casts twice a second, and up to ten with better worker's gloves.

Fishers cost 400 gold, and each next one costs 45% more. Each one adds 0.35 power every second, also while you are away. You can hire up to 200.

Move to the next water as soon as it opens

In each water, the cheapest fish are the most common. At fishing level 8, an average catch at the lake is worth about 48 gold. At the river, which opens at level 8, the only fish you can catch yet is the rainbow trout at 260. That is more than five times as much for the same taps. At level 15 the same happens again: an average river catch is worth about 450, while the sea gives tuna at 1,300. (The lake has eight fish, the river five and the sea five.) An upgraded rod shifts the odds toward the pricier fish in every water.

Rare catches, bait and kings

  • About 8% of catches are rare (plus 1% per rod tier) and land 2 to 4 fish at once. A legendary catch is much rarer and lands about 6 to 14.
  • Bait doubles both chances. Five potatoes, corn, apples or sweet potatoes make 25 baited catches. With auto-bait on, the game uses the crop you have most of.
  • Each water has one king: the golden carp (20,000) from fishing level 5, the river dragon (80,000) from 12 and the king of the deep (300,000) from 20. The chance is 0.08% per catch, and bait does not help.
  • About 1% of catches are a treasure chest instead of a fish, worth 300 gold per fishing level.

The fish album belongs to the family and is never lost. Every fish you have caught in all three rarities adds 4% to the price of all fish, and every king adds 10%.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the barn sad all evening. Without a rancher, the barn runs at a third of its speed while you sleep.
  • Breeding camels with a 20% chance.
  • Staying at the lake out of habit after the river opens.
  • Letting the shipping bin sell every potato and apple, then having no bait.

First generation vs later generations

Generation 1. Only the barn. Chickens and cows carry your income while the field is still small. Get the rancher before your first long break.

Generation 2 and after. The lake opens, and fishing becomes a third steady income next to crops and animals. The ranch perks start to pay off: «Happy animals» adds 20% production per level, «Family flock» starts you with two chickens per level, and «Livestock dealer» cuts animal prices by 10% per level. «Expert breeder» means a failed breeding never costs you the animals. The «Family cow» heirloom starts each generation with cows. «Strong arms» raises fishing power as well as mining. And the album keeps growing from one generation to the next, so every fish counts.
