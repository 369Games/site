Animals and fishing

The barn gives you income that does not care about the season. Every animal makes one product on a fixed timer, and a happy animal makes more. You unlock new animals by raising your ranching level, which grows as you collect products.

The rancher, hired in the village, looks after the animals and keeps them fully happy, and each barn expansion adds room for five more of every kind. Animals can also be bred for better lines; the beehive and the camel have named breeds of their own.

Fishing opens three waters as your fishing level rises. Each water has its own six fish, from common ones to rare catches worth thousands.
