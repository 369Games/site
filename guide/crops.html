Crops and seasons

There are 24 crops in UntoldLife: five for each of the four seasons, and four rare seeds that sit outside the seasons. A crop planted in its own season grows at full speed; out of season it pays a penalty, unless the plot is under a greenhouse.

The last column is the profit for one plot in one minute, counting the seed you have to buy again. It is the plain number before watering, tools, perks and villagers, all of which raise it. Use it to compare crops, not as a promise.

One simple rule for choosing a crop

Look at the profit column in the tables above, season by season. Inside every season, each new crop you unlock earns more per minute than the one before it. In spring the numbers go 21, 31, 33, 60 and 93 gold per minute, from turnip to broccoli. The other seasons climb the same way, and the rare seeds sit above all of them.

So the rule is easy: plant the newest crop of the current season that your farming level allows. The only thing that can stop you is gold. A field of eight strawberries needs 480 gold of seeds before the first harvest. If you are that short on gold, plant the cheaper crop for one round and switch when you can afford it.

Higher crops also level you faster. Every harvest gives farming XP equal to a fifth of the crop's base price: 2.4 XP for a turnip, 30 for a strawberry, 140 for a watermelon. Farming level 10 needs 18,797 XP in total. That is about 7,800 turnips, or about 630 strawberries.

How fast a plot really grows

The grow times in the tables are the plain times. In the game, three things change them.

  • Growth bonuses. The hoe adds 20% per tier, each farming level adds 2%, and each of Emily's hearts adds 3%. These add up together. Legacy bonuses, such as «Rich soil» at +25% per level, form a second group that multiplies the first one.
  • Water. A watered plot grows twice as fast. Every tier of the watering can adds another 0.25, up to ×3.5 with an adamantite can.
  • The season. A crop out of its season grows at half speed.

Worked example. A strawberry takes 90 seconds. With a copper hoe (+20%), farming level 6 (+10%) and Emily at 2 hearts (+6%), the plot grows 1.36 times faster: about 66 seconds. Water it with a copper can (×2.25) and it is ready in about 29 seconds. That is three times the speed of a bare plot, without any legacy perk.

When you are tapping by hand, water the slow crops first. Watering a turnip saves 10 seconds, but watering a watermelon saves 100.

Seasons in practice

A season lasts seven in-game days, about 17.5 real minutes, so it changes often. Three things follow from that:

  • Change the seed when the season changes. The planter replants the seed you have selected. If you leave last season's seed selected, the planter keeps planting a crop at half speed.
  • Crops in the ground slow down too. A spring crop still growing when summer starts finishes at half speed. For long crops such as grapes (4 min 20 s) or chestnuts (4 min 40 s), the start of the season is the best time to plant.
  • An off-season crop loses more than half its value. A turnip in summer takes 40 seconds and earns about 10.5 gold a minute. A cucumber, the summer crop of the same level, earns 24.5. That is more than twice as much.

Rain waters the whole field for a full day, and about one day in five is rainy, except in winter. Winter has snow, which waters nothing, so winter is when sprinklers help the most.

Emily's seasonal perk changes every season. Sometimes it is about off-season growth. Above 5 hearts it softens the half-speed penalty, but below 5 hearts it makes the penalty worse. Check her card in the village before you plant out of season on purpose.

Automation: which plots the helpers cover

The harvester, the planter and the sprinklers each cover a share of your field: 10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and then 100% at levels 1 to 5. They count from the first plot, and the number is rounded up. With 12 plots, a level 3 harvester covers the first 6. Any plot beyond that you still harvest by hand.

The greenhouse also works on the first plots: the first 4, 8 or 12, for 100,000, 300,000 and 1,000,000 gold. So the plots under glass are the same ones your helpers reach first.

«Plot assignment» (5,000 gold) lets you give each plot its own crop. The planter then replants that crop there, whatever seed is selected. One handy use: keep the greenhouse plots on a crop you like all year, and let the rest follow the season.

More than gold: what crops are also for

  • Double harvests. Each farming level gives a 1% chance to get two crops from one harvest. The «Old seed bag» heirloom adds 3% more.
  • Bait for fishing. Potatoes, corn, apples and sweet potatoes (one crop from each season) turn into bait: 5 of any one of them give 25 baited catches with double the chance of rare and legendary fish.
  • Food in the dungeon. Your fighter eats from storage, cheapest first. Locked items are never eaten and never sold.

Common mistakes

  • Keeping turnips after unlocking garlic and potatoes. Turnips only win in the first minutes, when seeds are the problem.
  • Buying sprinklers before the harvester and the planter. Sprinklers save taps, but the two helpers are what let the field run while you are away.
  • Selling every crop with the bin, then having nothing to use as bait. Lock a stack before you need it.

First generation vs later generations

Generations 1 and 2. You only have the twenty seasonal crops, so the farm follows the calendar. The seed switch every season and, later, the greenhouse are what keep the field at full speed.

Generation 3 and after. The four rare seeds open: saffron at farming level 10, dates at 13, mango at 16 and pineapple at 20. They grow in every season with no penalty. Saffron makes 220 gold a minute per plot, more than any seasonal crop, and pineapple makes about 460. With rare seeds the planter never needs a new seed, and the greenhouse matters much less.

Farming perks. «Rich soil» adds 25% growth per level, up to +250%. «Inherited land» starts you with two extra plots per level, up to 14. «Wisdom of the land» speeds up farming XP. «The golden seed» gives a chance of a golden crop worth ten times its price. Once you have spent 10 levels in the farming branch, «Eternal Seeds» means planting never costs seeds. With pineapple seeds at 2,200 gold each, that is a big saving.
