Crops and seasons

There are 24 crops in UntoldLife: five for each of the four seasons, and four rare seeds that sit outside the seasons. A crop planted in its own season grows at full speed; out of season it pays a penalty, unless the plot is under a greenhouse.

The last column is the profit for one plot in one minute, counting the seed you have to buy again. It is the plain number before watering, tools, perks and villagers, all of which raise it. Use it to compare crops, not as a promise.

Buildings that run the field

These are bought in the Village tab. Several of them have levels: each level covers more of your plots.

NameWhat it doesPrice
🧑‍🌾 HarvesterHarvests ready crops automatically300
📦 Shipping binSells every unlocked item at the end of each day600
👨‍🌾 PlanterReplants empty plots with the chosen seed1,500
🎯 Plot assignmentGive each plot a fixed crop for the planter to replant5,000
💦 SprinklersWaters the plots by itself15K
🏡 GreenhouseProtects 4 plots from the off-season penalty, and each upgrade adds 4 more (up to 12). The first plots in the field are the ones under the greenhouse.100K

Tip: early on, short crops such as turnip and cucumber are worth more than their price suggests, because you harvest them many times while a slow crop is still growing. Slow, expensive crops become the better choice once the harvester and planter cover the whole field.
