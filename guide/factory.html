The factory: the Industrial Age

The factory is the second half of UntoldLife. It opens when three things are true: the family has reached its tenth generation, 80% of the legacy tree's levels have been bought, and you can pay 1,000 legacy stars. From then on the valley has two worlds, the village and the factory, and you move between them with one button.

Unlike the village, the factory does not restart when you retire. It belongs to the family.

Fields and workers

Four fields gather raw materials by themselves: Clay pit, Stone mountain, Forest, Sand quarry. A crew of up to 200 workers is shared between them, and you decide how many stand in each field. The sand quarry opens later, with the glass and steel it feeds.

Furnaces

Furnaces turn raw materials into building materials. Copper, iron and silver ore come from the village mine, and coal is the fuel for most recipes. When a furnace is short of something, the game marks it in red and names what is missing.
