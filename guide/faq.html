Frequently asked questions

Is UntoldLife really free?

Yes. The whole game plays in the browser for free, with no purchases that give an advantage. A paid PC version without ads is planned for Steam.

Where is my save kept?

On your own device, in the browser's storage, written every 10 seconds. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it, so download a backup file now and then from the «More» tab. You can load that file on any device.

Can I continue on another device?

Yes, in two ways: move the backup file by hand, or sign in from the «More» tab to keep a copy of your save in your account.

Does the game keep going when I close it?

It does. When you return, the game works out what the valley produced while you were away, up to 12 hours. A legacy perk called «Sound sleep» extends this by six hours per level.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game is built for phones as much as for computers, and you can install it to your home screen from the «More» tab so it opens like an app.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic, English, Russian and Simplified Chinese. The language button is at the top of the game.

How long does one generation take?

The first one is the longest, since nothing is automated yet. Later generations go much faster because of legacy perks and heirlooms. How long to stay in a generation is your decision: retiring earlier gives fewer stars sooner.

When does the factory open?

At generation 10, once 80% of the legacy tree's levels are bought and you have 1,000 legacy stars to pay. See the factory page.

What is the weekly leaderboard?

A ranking that resets every week, together with shared village goals that all players push forward. Saves that have been edited are left out of it.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Use the feedback form inside the game, under «More», or write to contact@369games.net. Many changes in the update log began as a message from a player.

How is the game made?

By a small independent studio. We design and build it ourselves and use AI tools for parts of the code, the art and the music, and we say so openly.

Will my save survive updates?

Yes. Every update is written to carry older saves forward, and that is tested before each release.
