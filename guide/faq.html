Frequently asked questions

Is UntoldLife really free?

Yes. The whole game plays in the browser for free, with no purchases that give an advantage. A paid PC version without ads is planned for Steam.

Where is my save kept?

On your own device, in the browser's storage, written every 10 seconds. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it, so download a backup file now and then from the «More» tab. You can load that file on any device.

Can I continue on another device?

Yes, in two ways: move the backup file by hand, or sign in from the «More» tab to keep a copy of your save in your account.

Does the game keep going when I close it?

It does. When you return, the game works out what the valley produced while you were away, up to 12 hours. A legacy perk called «Sound sleep» extends this by six hours per level.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game is built for phones as much as for computers, and you can install it to your home screen from the «More» tab so it opens like an app.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic, English, Russian and Simplified Chinese. The language button is at the top of the game.

How long does one generation take?

The first one is the longest, since nothing is automated yet. Later generations go much faster because of legacy perks and heirlooms. How long to stay in a generation is your decision: retiring earlier gives fewer stars sooner.

When does the factory open?

At generation 10, once 80% of the legacy tree's levels are bought and you have 1,000 legacy stars to pay. See the factory page.

What is the weekly leaderboard?

A ranking that resets every week, together with shared village goals that all players push forward. Saves that have been edited are left out of it.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Use the feedback form inside the game, under «More», or write to contact@369games.net. Many changes in the update log began as a message from a player.

How is the game made?

By a small independent studio. We design and build it ourselves and use AI tools for parts of the code, the art and the music, and we say so openly.

Will my save survive updates?

Yes. Every update is written to carry older saves forward, and that is tested before each release.

What exactly do I need to retire?

Two things in the same generation: enough gold earned, and a deep enough dungeon floor. In the first three generations that is 1 million gold and floor 30. From the fourth generation the gold goal grows by ×1.7 a generation up to generation 10, then by ×1.3 up to generation 20, and by ×1.1 after that. The dungeon goal rises by three floors a generation after the third, and it is always at least 15 floors above the floor you started that generation on. The Legacy tab shows both goals and how close you are.

How are legacy stars worked out?

From three parts of the generation: the gold you earned, the dungeon floors you climbed in that life, and how far below floor 20 you dug in the mine. Each generation has a cap on stars: 500 up to generation 5, 900 up to 10, 1,500 up to 15, 2,400 up to 20 and 3,600 after that. The mine's share is capped at 500 on its own. The perk «Wisdom of generations» and the heirloom «Lucky amulet» raise both your stars and the cap. Earning more before you retire gives more stars, until you reach the cap.

What are generation tasks?

Three small tasks that change every generation, shown in the Legacy tab. Each finished task adds 5% of that generation's star cap to your reward when you retire, and finishing all three makes it 20%. This bonus comes on top of the cap, so it is worth finishing them even when you have already reached it.

Why can I not open some tabs?

In the first generation the tabs open one at a time. The Village and «This week» open with your first sale, the barn when you hire your first harvester, the mine at 1,000 gold earned, the smith after 20 rocks broken, the guild after your first tool upgrade, and Legacy at 25,000 gold earned. A locked tab tells you what it is waiting for. From the second generation everything is open from the start.

I cannot find the lake. Where is it?

Fishing opens in the second generation. The lake is first; the river opens at fishing level 8 and the sea at level 15. The fishing rod is upgraded at the smith like the other tools.

What happens if I plant a crop out of season?

It still grows, but at half speed. The greenhouse in the village protects four plots from this penalty, and each upgrade adds four more, up to 12. The four rare seeds (saffron, dates, mango and pineapple) open in the third generation and grow in every season.

Does watering really matter?

Yes. A watered plot grows twice as fast. On rainy days the rain waters the whole field for you, and once you build sprinklers they keep the plots watered every day.

Why does «Sell all unlocked» leave my ores behind?

Ores and gems start locked, because the smith needs them for upgrades. You can lock or unlock any item in the Market with its lock button. A locked item is never sold in bulk and never eaten as food in battle, which is also a good way to keep a gift for a villager.

When can I get married?

From the fifth generation. You need 10 hearts with a villager who can marry, and 50,000 gold. In the same generation you can also ask for a lifelong friend, for the same price. Both choices shape the family that inherits the farm.

When do challenge generations open, and are they required?

They open in the fifth generation and they are optional. When you retire you can choose a challenge for the next generation, such as «Endless winter» or «Slow time». Finishing its goal gives a permanent reward. Each challenge can be done again, and its reward grows every time.

Do I lose my tools when I retire?

Normally, yes: tools stay with the old life and the new generation starts again. The keystone perk «Heirloom Tools» changes that. Your tools pass down one tier lower, and legendary tools come back as adamantite.

Is there a faster way to dig and fish than tapping?

Hold the «Dig!» or «Cast!» button instead of tapping it again and again. The worker's gloves at the smith raise the speed up to 10 hits a second. For digging without you, hire miners; for fishing, hire fishers.

When does the week start over?

The weekly challenge and the leaderboards reset every Sunday at 00:00 Riyadh time, which is Saturday 21:00 UTC. The three village goals have their own daily rounds: crops start over at 06:00 UTC, mine rocks at 12:00 UTC and fish at 18:00 UTC.

How do village goal prizes work?

All signed-in players fill the same goal together. If the goal is reached when its round ends, everyone who helped gets extra speed in that area for seven days: the biggest helper +10%, second +7.5%, third +5%, and everyone else who gave something +2%. The next day's target is set from how much the village managed the day before, so it follows how active everyone is.

My numbers on the leaderboard look old. Why?

Your numbers are sent every 5 minutes while you play, or every 15 minutes if you have not touched the game for a while, and the boards themselves refresh about every 10 minutes. This keeps the game light. Give it a few minutes and your row will catch up.

Do I need a password for an account?

No. You can sign in with Google, or type your email and we send you a sign-in link. From the same panel in «More» you can sign out (your save stays on the device) or delete the account and its cloud save.
