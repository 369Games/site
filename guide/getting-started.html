Getting started: your first hour

UntoldLife starts small on purpose. You have 50 gold, a few empty plots and a bag of turnip seeds. The first hour is about getting the field to run without you, because everything else in the valley is paid for by the farm.

Step by step

  1. Plant and harvest. Tap an empty plot to plant the selected seed, or use «Plant all». A turnip takes 20 seconds. Tap a ready plot to harvest it, or use «Harvest all».
  2. Water the plots. Watered crops grow faster. Later, sprinklers do this for you.
  3. Sell at the market. Crops go to your storage; the Market tab turns them into gold. A shipping bin, once built, sells everything at the end of each day.
  4. Buy more plots and better seeds. Each new plot costs more than the last. Better seeds need a higher farming level, which you gain simply by harvesting.
  5. Hire help in the village. The harvester collects ready crops by itself and the planter replants empty plots. With both, the farm runs while you look at other places.
  6. Buy your first chickens. Animals give a steady income that does not depend on the season. Pet them to keep them happy.
  7. Open the mine. Press «Dig» to break rocks, or hire miners. Ores are sold for gold and are needed to upgrade your tools at the smith.
  8. Join the guild. Fight through the dungeon floor by floor and take quests to raise your rank from F to S.

Things worth knowing early

  • Each crop belongs to a season. Out of season it still grows, but with a penalty, so follow the season picker above the seeds.
  • The game saves by itself every 10 seconds on your device. You can also download a backup file from the «More» tab, and it is a good habit to do so.
  • When you close the page the valley keeps working for up to 12 hours. When you come back you get a summary of what was earned.
  • A step-by-step guide inside the game points at the next thing to do. You can skip it at any time.
  • Do not rush to spend on everything. The helpers that automate the field pay for themselves faster than anything else in the first generation.

Your first retirement

Your farmer ages as the seasons pass. When the Legacy tab shows that you can retire, you are not losing the farm: your child begins a new life with legacy stars to spend on permanent perks. The first retirement is the moment the game opens up, so do not put it off for long. The Generations and legacy page explains what carries over.
