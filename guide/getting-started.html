Getting started: your first hour

UntoldLife starts small on purpose. You have 50 gold, a few empty plots and a bag of turnip seeds. The first hour is about getting the field to run without you, because everything else in the valley is paid for by the farm.

Step by step

  1. Plant and harvest. Tap an empty plot to plant the selected seed, or use «Plant all». A turnip takes 20 seconds. Tap a ready plot to harvest it, or use «Harvest all».
  2. Water the plots. Watered crops grow faster. Later, sprinklers do this for you.
  3. Sell at the market. Crops go to your storage; the Market tab turns them into gold. A shipping bin, once built, sells everything at the end of each day.
  4. Buy more plots and better seeds. Each new plot costs more than the last. Better seeds need a higher farming level, which you gain simply by harvesting.
  5. Hire help in the village. The harvester collects ready crops by itself and the planter replants empty plots. With both, the farm runs while you look at other places.
  6. Buy your first chickens. Animals give a steady income that does not depend on the season. Pet them to keep them happy.
  7. Open the mine. Press «Dig» to break rocks, or hire miners. Ores are sold for gold and are needed to upgrade your tools at the smith.
  8. Join the guild. Fight through the dungeon floor by floor and take quests to raise your rank from F to S.

Things worth knowing early

  • Each crop belongs to a season. Out of season it still grows, but with a penalty, so follow the season picker above the seeds.
  • The game saves by itself every 10 seconds on your device. You can also download a backup file from the «More» tab, and it is a good habit to do so.
  • When you close the page the valley keeps working for up to 12 hours. When you come back you get a summary of what was earned.
  • A step-by-step guide inside the game points at the next thing to do. You can skip it at any time.
  • Do not rush to spend on everything. The helpers that automate the field pay for themselves faster than anything else in the first generation.

Your first retirement

When you reach the goal of your generation (enough gold earned and a deep enough dungeon floor) the Legacy tab lets you retire, and you are not losing the farm: your child begins a new life with legacy stars to spend on permanent perks. The first retirement is the moment the game opens up, so do not put it off for long. The Generations and legacy page explains what carries over.

Your first ten minutes, with real numbers

You start in spring with 50 gold and four empty plots. Four turnip seeds cost 20 gold. Each turnip sells for 12, so one round of four plots leaves you about 28 gold of profit. That sounds tiny, and it is, but it doubles quickly if you tap the plots while they grow.

Tapping a plot that is already growing waters it. A watered crop grows twice as fast, so a turnip takes 10 seconds instead of 20. The water lasts for one crop only: when you harvest, the plot dries out, and the next seed needs another tap. Watering also only speeds up the time that is left, so tap right after planting, not halfway through.

Your first extra plots are cheap, then they climb fast. Each new plot costs 1.75 times the one before:

Every harvest gives farming XP equal to one fifth of the crop's base price. A turnip gives 2.4 XP, a potato 8, a strawberry 30. Level 2 needs 25 XP (about eleven turnips), and level 3 needs 166 XP in total. Each farming level adds 2% growth speed and a 1% chance to harvest two crops instead of one, so levels matter even after the new seeds are open.

How the tabs open in your first generation

In the first generation the game does not show you everything at once. Each tab opens when you reach a small goal, and a short window tells you what it is for. From the second generation on, every tab is open from the start.

A good order for the first hour

  1. Turnips, watered by hand. Plant all four plots, tap each one to water it, harvest, sell. Buy the 5th and 6th plots as soon as you can.
  2. Sell once, then buy the harvester (300 gold). The first sale opens the village. The harvester does two jobs: it collects ready crops for you, and it opens the barn.
  3. Buy chickens right away. This is the biggest jump of the early game. A chicken costs 150 and lays an egg worth 50 every 30 seconds. A happy chicken works 1.5 times faster, so it makes about 150 gold a minute and pays for itself in about one minute. An unwatered turnip plot makes about 21 gold a minute. One happy chicken earns as much as seven of those plots.
  4. Fill the barn's first six spots. Each chicken costs 25% more than the last: 150, 188, 234, 293, 366 and 458, or 1,689 gold for all six. The barn holds six of each animal at the start.
  5. Open the mine at 1,000 gold earned. Break 20 rocks to open the smith. The first upgrade of any tool costs 500 gold and 20 copper.
  6. Buy the planter (1,500) and the shipping bin (600). Now the field replants and sells itself.

The clock, the weather and the seasons

One day in the valley lasts 2.5 real minutes. A season is seven days, so it lasts about 17.5 minutes, and a full year about 70 minutes. This is faster than most players expect: you will see all four seasons in your first hour or so.

Each morning the game rolls the weather. Outside winter, about one day in five is rainy, and rain waters every plot in the field for the whole day. Winter has snow instead of rain, and snow does not water anything. So in winter your own taps (or sprinklers) are the only water.

The «Water all» button is greyed out at first. It works once the smith has upgraded your watering can to copper.

Before you close the page

While the game is closed, it replays the time you were away (up to 12 hours), but only the things that run by themselves keep working. A short checklist:

  • The field. Without a harvester, ready crops just sit in the plot. Without a planter, harvested plots stay empty. Both helpers only cover part of the field at their first levels, counting from the first plot.
  • The barn. Happiness falls on its own. From full to empty takes about 14 minutes, and a sad barn produces only a third of what a happy one does. That happens while you are away too, unless you have hired the rancher.
  • Selling. The shipping bin sells everything that is not locked at the start of each in-game day, every 2.5 minutes, also while you are away.
  • Ores stay safe. Ores and gems are locked by default, so the bin and «Sell all unlocked» will not touch them. You need them at the smith. You can unlock any item in the market if you want to sell it.

If you only switch to another browser tab for a few minutes, the game catches up quietly, without opening the summary window.

Common mistakes in the first generation

  • Buying plot after plot. The 12th plot costs 4,021 gold and the 13th 7,037. Long before that, the same gold buys several chickens or a cow that earns far more.
  • Forgetting the barn. Animals do not need seeds or water, but they do need petting until you have a rancher. Each tap on «Pet» adds 25 happiness to that kind of animal.
  • Planting last season's seed. When the season changes, crops from the old season grow at half speed. Change the selected seed, because the planter uses it too.
  • Selling food you will need. In the dungeon your fighter eats crops, animal products and fish from your storage, cheapest first, and never touches locked items.

First generation vs later generations

Generation 1. Your goal is to earn 1,000,000 gold during this generation and to reach floor 30 of the dungeon. The Legacy tab shows both bars. The gold goal stays at 1 million for the first three generations and grows from the fourth.

What starts over. Every new generation begins again with 50 gold, four plots, spring, level 1 skills, basic tools and no village buildings. That is normal, and it goes much faster the second time.

What the family keeps. Legacy stars and perks, heirlooms, achievements (each one adds 1% to gold and XP), the fish album and the pets all stay with the family.

Generation 2. All tabs are open and the lake opens for fishing. Perks that give you a better start, such as extra plots, starting gold or the workers, apply right away if you buy them in the first two in-game days (about five real minutes). After that, they wait for the next generation. So spend your stars as soon as the new generation begins.

Generation 3. The four rare seeds open. They grow in every season with no penalty, and they are the most profitable crops in the game. See Crops and seasons.

Retire a little later, not much later. The gold part of your legacy stars grows with the square root of what you earned: before other bonuses, 1 million gives 3 stars and 4 million gives 6. Doubling your earnings does not double the stars, so once the goal is met and the next gold feels slow, it is usually time to retire.
