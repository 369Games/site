The guild and the dungeon

The guild is the fighting side of the valley. You go down the dungeon one floor at a time, and every few floors a boss guards the way. Fights run by themselves; your part is the gear you bring, the food you carry and when you choose to go deeper.

Guild quests give guild points, and points raise your rank. A higher rank opens deeper floors and better quests.

Gear

You carry six pieces of gear: helmet, amulet, sword, armor, shield and boots. All of them are upgraded at the smith with ore from the mine. The inn in the village doubles how fast you recover, and the kitchen makes food heal twice as much in battle.

Legendary pets

Deep in the dungeon you can find legendary eggs. An egg hatches into one of three companions, and each one strengthens a different side of your fighter as it grows from baby to adult.

  • 🐉 the dragon: attack
  • 🦄 the unicorn: defense
  • 🦅 the griffin: Power
