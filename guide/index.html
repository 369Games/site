UntoldLife game guide

UntoldLife is a free idle farming game that runs in your browser. You inherit your grandfather's farm in Echo Valley, plant the first turnips by hand, and slowly turn one small field into a working valley: a barn, a mine, a lake, an adventurers' guild, a smith and a village of people who remember your family.

What sets it apart is the family line. Your farmer grows old. When the time comes you retire and hand everything to your child, who starts again with legacy stars, heirlooms and permanent perks earned by the generations before. After ten generations the valley is ready for its Industrial Age: a factory with fields, furnaces and workshops, public services for the village, and finally the First Train.

This guide is written by the people who make the game. Every table on these pages is produced from the game's own data, so the numbers you read here are the numbers you will see when you play.

The game at a glance

  • 24 crops across four seasons and a set of rare seeds, 7 animals, 18 fish and 8 ores.
  • 29 legacy perks in six branches, 12 heirlooms and 6 challenge generations.
  • 54 achievements, a weekly leaderboard and shared village goals.
  • Progress continues while the game is closed, for up to 12 hours (more with a legacy perk).
  • Available in Arabic, English, Russian and Chinese, on phones and computers, with no download and no sign-up.
