Generations and legacy

A life in UntoldLife is one generation. Your farmer ages with the seasons, and when the time comes you retire from the Legacy tab. The farm, the gold and the tools stay with that life; what moves on is the family: legacy stars, heirlooms, and every perk the family has ever bought.

Legacy stars are earned from what the generation achieved. You spend them in the legacy tree below, and the perks are permanent. This is why each generation moves faster than the one before, and why retiring early in the first generations is usually right.
