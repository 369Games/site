The mine and the smith

The mine goes down floor by floor. Press «Dig» to break rocks yourself, or hire miners to dig for you. Every five rocks broken on your deepest floor take you one floor further down. Deeper ores are worth more, and you need them to upgrade your tools.

How deep you can go depends on your pickaxe. A basic pickaxe stops at floor 12; each upgrade pushes the limit further, and the best pickaxe has no limit at all.

How deep each pickaxe digs

The pickaxe has seven levels. Their floor limits are 12, 28, 48, 75, 105, 150, and the seventh has none.

The smith

The smith upgrades twelve tools. Each one has six upgrades, paid for with gold and the ore of that level, so the mine and the smith feed each other.

  • 🌾 Hoe
  • 🚿 Watering can
  • ⛏️ Pickaxe
  • 🗡️ Sword
  • 🛡️ Armor
  • ⛑️ Helmet
  • 🔰 Shield
  • 📿 Amulet
  • 👢 Boots
  • 🎣 Fishing rod
  • 🧤 Worker's gloves
  • 🐑 Shepherd's crook

At the top of the smith is the forge, where a fully upgraded tool can be forged into its legendary form. Every forging rolls a bonus between 5% and 100%, and you can forge again to roll a better one. The perfect 100% roll has a 0.5% chance, and the 300th try on a tool is guaranteed, so nobody is left unlucky forever. The game shows how many tries are left under the forge button.
