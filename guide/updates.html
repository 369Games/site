Update log

UntoldLife has been updated 105 times since its first beta on 2026-11-01. This is the same list the game shows behind the version button, newest first.

1.22.4 2026-11-14

A tidier market and village

  • 🛒 Market cards are wider and tidy: the name, price, quantity and sell buttons sit on one line instead of piling on top of each other. And the villagers' hearts no longer run outside their card.

1.22.3 2026-11-14

A quicker way in

  • 🚪 A lighter first screen: the “Enter the village” button now comes right after the letter, and your name, character and language sit below it as options. Plus a loading screen with the logo instead of an empty page on a slow connection.

1.22.2 2026-11-13

Notifications while you're away

  • 🔔 Optional notifications under “More”: you get one when a ship is back, a contract is ready, the factory stops, or you can retire, while you're in another window. They work as long as the game is open in the background.

1.22.1 2026-11-12

Install the game as an app

  • 📲 An “Install as an app” button under “More”: the game sits on your home screen or desktop and opens in its own window. On iPhone it tells you how, from the Share button.

1.22 2026-11-12

An easier factory, and a train with conditions

  • 👷 The factory’s field crew is now 200 instead of 150 (for the sand quarry), at the same total price: all 200 cost what the 150 did. Workers you already had are converted by the gold you paid, so 150 become 200. Workers’ housing now gives +15 per level.
  • ⏫ A “To the max” button in the factory: hire every worker your gold covers in one press, and the same for each kind of furnace and bench. No more 150 presses after every renewal.
  • 🚂 The last three train stages now ask for the invention map: the locomotive 60% of it, the wagons 80%, and the first journey the whole map (counted in patents spent). A stage built before the update stays built.
  • 🎯 Legendary forging now has a guarantee: try number 300 on the same tool is a sure golden 100%. The chance before that is unchanged (0.5%), and the count shows under the forge button and stays with you across generations.
  • ⚙️ The Industrial Age is closer: 80% of the legacy tree’s levels is enough, instead of the whole tree (with generation 10 and 1,000 stars). The condition shows how many levels are left.
  • 🔎 A stopped furnace or bench now names the material it's out of, and marks it in red in the “Needs” row.
  • 🪵 Fix: a furnace whose output was nearly full used to bank held-back work, then burn its inputs in one go (your wood dropping by hundreds per second for no clear reason). It no longer banks anything.

1.21.2 2026-11-11

A tidier tab bar on phones

  • 📱 On phones the tab bar now shows six tabs instead of three (the picture on top, the name under it), and the open tab moves to the middle by itself so you don't have to hunt for it.
  • 👆 The − and + worker buttons are bigger on phones, and the generations road is two columns so the page is shorter.
  • 🎨 The train and harbor art is in: the five stages, the harbor in its four looks, the voyages, foreign goods and their upgrades, two medals, and a picture for the ending screen.
  • 💡 A short card the first time you open the deep mine, the services, the inventions, the train and the harbor, and one when the quarries open.

1.21.1 2026-11-11

Services worth building, sharper generation tasks

  • 🎛️ Income from the services (power, water and taxes) is now 50 times bigger. They used to pay under 1% of what contracts pay; now building them makes a difference. The gold price of their next levels is 10 times higher to balance the new income; what you already built stays yours.
  • 🎯 Generation tasks are now sized from what you did in your last generation instead of the average of all generations, so you no longer get a task that's done in fifteen minutes. This starts with your next generation.
  • 🧹 A tidier event log: the “found a rare gem” and “golden crop” lines are gone, because they were flooding the log. The gems and the gold still come as before.

1.21 2026-11-11

A bigger invention map

  • 🗾 The invention map now has 15 inventions in three columns: the engineer and the mine, services and furnaces, and storage and trade. The cards are smaller, each invention has dots that show its level, and the top shows how many patents you've spent and how many are left.
  • ⚫ Clean coal: coal furnaces give 3 instead of 2. 🏭 Furnaces that stay: you start a renewal with furnaces and benches ready. 👷 Ready workers: you start a renewal with workers already placed.
  • ⛏️ Fixed drills: drills are no longer reset by a renewal. And a fourth drill: one more slot in the deep mine, for 200 steam engines.
  • 📦 Giant warehouse: the warehouse can reach level 12 (4× the capacity). 🚢 A fifth ship in the harbor.
  • 🎨 An icon of its own for each of the seven new inventions.

1.20 2026-11-11

The deep mine

  • ⛏️ A new factory tab: the deep mine. Up to three drills that run by themselves, and you pick an ore for each: coal for the factory warehouse, or copper, iron or silver for the village stock the furnaces draw on.
  • 🐢 Their output is slow and steady: a drill starts at 2 ores a second and reaches 30 at level 10. Changing the ore is free, any time.
  • 🛠️ The first drill costs 100 machines, the second 500 machines, and the third wants 50 steam engines, so it comes after the grand station. Drills are reset by a factory renewal.
  • 🎨 Art for the three drills and the tab icon, and a new achievement: King of the Drills.

1.19.1 2026-11-11

The train asks for quarry goods

  • 🚂 From the locomotive on, the train's stages now ask for quarry goods: the locomotive wants steel and steam engines, the wagons want beams and windows, and the first journey wants glass and engines. The gold for those three stages has doubled (40T, 60T and 100T).
  • 📜 The wait for a new contract and for a swap is now 10 minutes, and the engineer's contract trader cuts 30 seconds a level, down to 5 minutes.
  • 🔒 The sand quarry, the glass and steel furnaces and the new benches now show from the start with a lock, so you know what's waiting after the grand station.
  • 🎨 New art for sand, glass, steel, the window, the beam and the steam engine, the sand quarry in its four looks, and the new continent voyage.

1.19 2026-11-11

The quarries: glass and steel

  • 🟡 The grand station (the second stage of the train) opens the quarries: a fourth field, the sand quarry, and two new furnaces for glass and steel.
  • 🔧 Three new workshop benches: windows (glass and planks), steel beams (steel and nails), and a steam engine made from a machine, beams and windows. Contracts start asking for them as soon as you make them.
  • 🌍 A fourth harbor voyage: the new continent. 12 hours, it wants engines, windows and steel, and it comes back with the biggest reward and twice the foreign goods.
  • 👷 “Even split” and “Smart split” now count the sand quarry with the other fields.

1.18 2026-11-11

Generation tasks

  • 🎯 Every generation now has three tasks of its own, and they change with each retirement: harvesting, animal products, mining, monsters, guild quests, fishing, a floor deeper than the retirement goal, or more gold. Find them in the Legacy tab.
  • ⭐ Each task you finish adds 5% of the generation's star cap to your stars at retirement, and all three together add 20%. The bonus is added on top of the cap, so it helps even when you've reached it.
  • 📈 The tasks grow with you: they ask for 60% of what your family did per generation on average, so you never get an impossible task or a trivial one.

1.17.1 2026-11-11

The generations road

  • 🗺️ In the Legacy tab: the “generations road”. Every stop in order, from the second generation to the game's finale: the lake, rare crops, the depths, marriage and challenges, guild titles, the Industrial Age, factory renewal, the harbor and the First Train.
  • 🎯 Your next stop is highlighted in gold, and the Industrial Age's conditions are listed under it with your progress on each, from the first generation. (Thanks to the player who suggested it)

1.17 2026-11-11

The harbor: ship voyages

  • ⚓ A new factory tab: the Harbor. It opens when you build the grand station (the second stage of the First Train), so you have both rails and a harbor. Load your ships from the factory warehouse and send them on voyages of 1, 4 or 8 real hours, which carry on while the game is closed.
  • 🧳 Every ship comes back with gold and foreign goods, a new currency that buys five permanent upgrades: contract gold, voyage speed, more goods, factory speed and village gold.
  • 🚢 Each harbor level makes the ship's cargo and its reward bigger, and you get a new ship at levels 4, 7 and 10. The harbor, its ships and its upgrades aren't reset by a factory renewal.
  • 🏅 Two new achievements: First Voyage and Master of the Seas.
  • 🎵 New music: a track of its own for the factory, and a piece for the First Train ending.
  • 🔕 Sounds are calmer: things your workers cause (a golden crop, a gem, a level-up) no longer make a sound unless they follow a press of yours. To bring them back, turn on “Automatic event sounds” in the sound settings.
  • 🔔 The factory now has its own sounds: delivering a contract, building, the town hall, furnaces and benches, hiring, the engineer, inventions, renewal, the train and the ships.

1.16 2026-11-10

The First Train: the game's finale

  • 🚂 A new factory tab: the First Train. A project in five stages (the main line, the grand station, the locomotive, the wagons, the first journey), each needing gold and materials in large amounts.
  • 🔁 Your progress on the train isn't reset by a factory renewal, and the last stage needs 50 patents.
  • 🎉 When it's complete you've finished UntoldLife: an ending screen with your stats, the “First Train” achievement, and a permanent reward: the factory 20% faster and +10% gold from every source. The game goes on afterwards.

1.15.4 2026-11-10

An easier start and a tidier phone layout

  • 🌐 A new player now gets the game in their browser's language straight away (Arabic, English, Russian or Chinese), and the name at the start is clearly optional.
  • 📱 The factory stock is three columns on phones, so you reach the furnaces without a long scroll.
  • 🔒 Copper, iron and silver lock themselves in the market once you have a factory, so they can't be sold by mistake.

1.15.3 2026-11-10

Furnace control and smart worker split

  • ⚒️ Every furnace and bench now has − and + to choose how many of them run, and the pause button stops them all and keeps your choice.
  • 👷 The fields have a “Smart split” button: it spreads workers by what the running furnaces and benches burn, and “Auto” redoes it every 30 seconds.

1.15.2 2026-11-10

A cap on contract size

  • 📜 Contracts grow 12% with each one you deliver, up to 300 contracts (×37 in size), then they stay there. The biggest possible contract pays about 5.3T, or 10.7T with a full contract trader.

1.15.1 2026-11-10

Smarter village goals

  • 🏘️ The daily village goal now adjusts itself: 75% of the previous round's total, and never below the old number. The more of you play, the bigger the goal, so it really takes teamwork now.

1.15 2026-11-10

Factory guide and achievements

  • 📖 A short guide the first time you enter the factory: hire a worker, buy a coal furnace, upgrade the town hall, build the rails, buy a bench and deliver your first contract. You can restart it from “More” while in the factory.
  • 🌙 When you come back, the “while you were away” summary now shows what the factory made and how much gold the services brought in.
  • 🏅 13 new factory achievements: contracts, the town hall, the rails and the boat, workers, renewals, services and the invention map. Each one gives +1% gold and XP like the others.
  • 🎨 New icons: the hourglass for countdowns, swap, celebration, the production gauge and renewal.

1.14 2026-11-10

Countdowns in the factory

  • ⏳ Next to every missing material in buildings, upgrades and contracts, you now see how long until you have it at your current production speed, and the same for gold.
  • 📦 Every field, furnace and bench now shows how long until its output fills the warehouse.
  • ⏳ Countdowns now use the last minute of production and tick down second by second instead of jumping; for the first 20 seconds they say “Working it out...”. The time now has its own line under the material.
  • ⏳ If the furnaces eat a material faster than it comes in, the countdown now says “Stock runs out in...” in red, and when it grows it says “Warehouse full in...” in green. Long waits now show in days.

1.13.9 2026-11-10

Furnaces make double, and cut stone has a use

  • ⚫ Every furnace now gives double output for the same materials: 2 coal, 2 bricks, 2 cut stones, and 2 copper, iron or silver ingots instead of 1.
  • 📌 Nails now need an iron ingot and cut stone instead of coal, so cut stone has a use and coal isn't in everything.

1.13.8 2026-11-10

Mine XP now grows with depth

  • ⛏️ Each rock's XP now follows its hardness, with a bonus for depth. Floor 1 used to give 40 times the XP of the deepest floor; now the deeper you go, the faster you level: the deepest floors are about 16 times faster than floor 1.

1.13.7 2026-11-10

Workshop recipes that make sense

  • 🔧 Workshop benches now make more than one piece: 4 wood make 2 planks, an iron ingot and a coal make 3 nails, 2 iron ingots and a copper ingot make 2 gears, and 2 copper ingots and 2 bricks make 2 pipes.

1.13.6 2026-11-10

Cheaper furnaces and town hall

  • 🏭 Furnace and workshop bench prices now rise ×1.3 each instead of ×1.6, so the 33 the town hall allows are within reach: the 33rd coal furnace costs about 221B instead of 170T.
  • 🏤 Every town hall upgrade now costs half: half the gold and half the materials.

1.13.5 2026-11-09

The invention map

  • 🗺️ The invention tree is now called the invention map, and the town hall has its own icon in the text.
  • 🏆 The weekly leaderboard now refreshes every 10 minutes instead of 2.

1.13.4 2026-11-09

The star cap went up

  • ⭐ The legacy star cap per generation now starts at 500 and climbs to 3,600: generations 1–5: 500, 6–10: 900, 11–15: 1,500, 16–20: 2,400, and 21 and up: 3,600. Wisdom of Generations and the lucky amulet raise it further.
  • ⛏️ The mine's star cap is now 500 instead of 200.

1.13.3 2026-11-09

Heirlooms don't change when you cancel

  • 🏺 The heirloom choices at retirement are now fixed for each generation. If you cancel and open retirement again (or reload the page), you get the same pieces.

1.13.2 2026-11-09

The lucky amulet raises the cap

  • 📿 The lucky amulet now raises the per-generation star cap along with the stars (+5% per level), like Wisdom of Generations in the tree. Before, its bonus was wasted once you hit the cap.

1.13.1 2026-11-09

Golden products count for requests

  • 🥛 If all your animals have turned golden, you can still give gifts and hand in guild deliveries: golden milk counts as milk, and the same goes for every product. The game uses the plain ones first, then the golden ones. (Thanks to Abu Mubarak for spotting it)

1.13 2026-11-09

Factory skyline and village electricity

  • 🏭 When you enter the factory, the banner at the top turns into an industrial town: factories, chimneys, a train crossing the bridge, and the town hall palace on the hill. At night the windows and furnaces light up.
  • 🔌 Once you build the power company, the village night changes: street lamps along the paths and the bridge, and every house lit up.
  • 🎫 Patents now have their own icon (a certificate with a gold seal) instead of the contract one.

1.12.3 2026-11-09

Services and inventions art

  • 🎨 22 new pictures: the power company, the water works and the tax office with four looks each that grow with them, icons for the Services and Inventions tabs, and an icon for every invention in the tree.

1.12.2 2026-11-09

The invention tree is now a tree

  • 🌳 Inventions are now a skill tree: public services on top, under it strong foundation, stronger grid and ready warehouse, and each invention opens the one below it. Thrifty factories sits under stronger grid and needs it complete (5/5).
  • 🔒 The Services tab now shows from the start with a lock, so you know what you're working toward.

1.12.1 2026-11-09

New invention: thrifty factories

  • ♻️ A new invention in the tree for 40 patents: all furnaces and workshop benches give the same output for half the materials.

1.12 2026-11-09

Inventions and public services

  • 🔬 A new factory tab: Inventions. Once the town hall is a palace (level 10), you can renew the factory: it starts over and you get 📜 patents for how far you got. The rails and the boat don't reset, so the village keeps its discounts.
  • 🌳 The invention tree: public services, strong foundation (the town hall starts higher), ready warehouse, engineer's notebook (his upgrades stay), fourth trader (a fourth contract slot), improved machines and a stronger grid.
  • ⚡ Public services: the power company, water and sewers, and the tax office. They bring in gold every second with no market, stay with you after a renewal, and their income grows with the village gold multiplier.

1.11.2 2026-11-09

Building and field art

  • 🎨 24 new pictures: the rails, the boat, the warehouse, the clay pit, the stone mountain and the forest, each with four looks that grow with it at levels 4, 7 and 10.

1.11.1 2026-11-09

Town hall art

  • 🏛️ The town hall now has its four pictures: a small house, a great hall, a grand civic hall, and a palace at level 10.
  • 🏗️ The rails, the boat, the warehouse and the fields now have four looks too, changing at levels 4, 7 and 10. Their pictures are coming soon.

1.11 2026-11-09

Town hall

  • 🏛️ A new building in the city: the town hall. It's the main building, and no building (rails, boat, warehouse) or field can go above its level. The levels you have now stay, but you can't raise them until the town hall catches up.
  • 🏭 Each town hall level allows 3 more furnaces and 3 more benches of each kind: 3 at level 0 up to 33 at level 10. Furnaces you already have stay.
  • 🏰 The town hall grows with its level: a small house, then a great hall, then a grand civic hall, and at level 10 a palace.
  • 📜 Contracts moved to the city. The wait after a delivery is now 6 minutes, and the contract trader cuts 18 seconds a level, down to 3 minutes. You have two swaps, and each comes back after the same time.

1.10.6 2026-11-08

Contracts give blueprints again

  • 📐 In the previous update contracts paid gold but no blueprints, and the contract counter stopped. Fixed, and you get 15 blueprints as a gift to make up for it.
  • 📊 The factory's material numbers now sit in a fixed grid, so the page no longer moves when they change.

1.10.5 2026-11-08

“Edited” stamp fix

  • 🧪 Contract gold wasn't counted in your earnings, so the game thought the save had been edited from outside and stamped it. Fixed, and the stamp is removed from every save that got it because of contracts.

1.10.4 2026-11-08

The warehouse no longer asks for its full capacity

  • 📦 No upgrade asks for exactly the warehouse capacity anymore (furnaces keep eating from it, so it never reaches the cap). Expanding the warehouse now asks a little less than its current capacity, for example 30,000 instead of 32,000, and the same goes for the rails and the boat.

1.10.3 2026-11-08

Cheaper factory workers

  • 👷 A factory field worker's price now rises 7% per worker instead of 30%. You can reach 50 workers and make use of workers' housing up to 150.

1.10.2 2026-11-08

Workshop art and contract wait

  • 🎨 Art for the products, blueprints, the Workshop tab, contracts, the engineer and its upgrades.
  • ⏳ After you deliver a contract, its slot waits 3 minutes for a new one. Swapping a contract is now free and any time, with no wait.

1.10.1 2026-11-08

Contracts now bring in gold

  • 💰 Contract gold is much bigger now, and the further down the chain a request is (ingots, products, machines), the bigger its reward. Contracts already on your board have their rewards updated too.

1.10 2026-11-08

Factory: the Workshop

  • 🔧 A new factory tab: the Workshop. Benches turn bricks and ingots into products: wooden planks, nails, gears, pipes, and machines made from all four.
  • 📜 Contracts: 3 trader requests at a time. Deliver them for gold and 📐 blueprints, and each one you deliver makes the next bigger. Don't like a contract? Swap it once every 5 minutes.
  • 👷 Engineer: permanent factory upgrades for blueprints and products: faster furnaces, richer fields, a faster workshop, workers' housing (+10 workers per level) and a contract trader (more gold).

1.9.1 2026-11-08

Factory: the warehouse matters now

  • 📦 Warehouse capacity starts at 1,000 and doubles with every upgrade. Rails and boat levels now need far more materials and ingots (gold is unchanged), so you have to expand the warehouse to reach the high levels.
  • 📈 Next to every material you see its net rate per second: green when it rises, red when the furnaces use more than is made.

1.9 2026-11-08

Industrial Age (phase one)

  • ⚙️ A new world opens once you finish the village: generation 10, the whole legacy tree at max (except Blessing of the Valley) and 1,000 legacy stars. Its card shows in the Legacy tab from generation 7.
  • ⛰️ Fields: the clay pit, the stone mountain and the forest, with workers you assign yourself. 🏭 Furnaces: coal, bricks, cut stone, and copper, iron and silver ingots from the village's metals.
  • 🏙️ City: rails make mine workers cheaper and the boat makes fishers cheaper, up to 90% off with much slower price growth. The worker cap is now 200 in the mine and 200 at the lake.
  • 🔁 The village and the factory always run together. A button at the end of the tabs moves you between them, with a red dot when the other world needs you.

1.8 2026-11-08

Legendary saddle, titles and auto-forge

  • 🏇 In the guild, under Legendary pets: craft one saddle for 100 million gold and put it on one pet (moving it is free). The saddle stays with the family for every generation.
  • 🐉 Saddled dragon: two floors down instead of one each clear. 🦅 Griffin: double monster materials. 🦄 Unicorn: 3 active titles next to your name instead of one.
  • 📈 The saddled pet earns the same XP as your fights and levels up: +0.01% per level (dragon attack, unicorn defense, griffin hit speed). Every pet's base bonus still works.
  • 🎖️ Title change: a title's bonus now only works while it's active next to your name. One active title, or 3 if the unicorn wears the saddle. Pick yours in the guild with “Turn on next to your name”.
  • ⚒️ At Jack the smith: the “Auto-forge” upgrade for 1 billion gold. Pick a legendary tool and the bonus you want, and it reforges by itself until it gets there or the ores run out. Bought once, it stays with the family.

1.7.15 2026-11-08

New icons

  • 🎨 27 icons painted in the game's style instead of phone emoji: buttons, headings, fishing spots, watering and planting, the ✅ marks and more.
  • 🔒 In the market: an open chest with a green mark means it can be sold, a closed chest with a red mark means it's locked. Plus new icons for the dungeon buttons, mercenary skills, pause and Support us.

1.7.14 2026-11-08

Buy animals in bulk

  • 🔢 In the barn, next to the buy button: a “Quantity” button. Type how many animals you want and see the full price before buying, with a “Max” button for the most your gold and space allow.

1.7.13 2026-11-08

Hunts are a challenge now

  • ⚔️ The power a hunt needs is now 50%–150% of your own power, instead of a small fixed number by rank. So some hunts are 100% and some make you choose.
  • 🦅 Your power here is counted without the griffin and the “Village Legend” title, so they really make hunts easier. Rewards haven't changed.

1.7.12 2026-11-08

Wisdom of Generations raises the cap

  • 🌳 “Wisdom of Generations” now raises the per-generation legacy star cap by the same percentage. For example at level 3 (+30%) a cap of 500 becomes 650. The mine's cap (200) hasn't changed.

1.7.11 2026-11-08

Eggs are for players who play

  • 🥚 Legendary pet eggs now only drop while the game is open and on screen. If it's closed or on another tab, the egg chance is zero.
  • 🐾 The pets you have stay, and they keep growing as usual even while you're away.

1.7.10 2026-11-07

Hunt reward ceiling

  • 📈 The hunt reward multiplier grows with the generation only up to ×200 (reached at generation 16), instead of without limit. High generations were getting hunts worth tens of billions.

1.7.9 2026-11-07

Guild titles

  • 🎖️ Once you reach rank S in the guild, “Guild titles” open: 10 titles. Pick one and every guild point after that goes to it.
  • Each title needs 50,000 points; once it's full it's yours for good with its bonus, and it stays with the family every generation. You can show any title you own next to your name.
  • 💰 Monster Hunter • ⚔️ Blade of the Guild • 🛡️ Valley Warden • ❤️ Iron Heart • 📖 Wise Warrior • 📜 Master of Quests • ⚡ Lightning • 🥚 Tamer of Legends • 🍽️ Ally of the Wounded • 👑 Village Legend.
  • 🐾 Pets grow more on harder floors: fitting counts as one floor, hard as two, very hard as 4 and deadly as 6. Easy only counts on the highest floor you've reached this generation (that is, while opening new floors).

1.7.8 2026-11-07

Pets grow through challenge

  • 🐾 Legendary pets now grow by the difficulty of the floor you clear: easy doesn't count, fitting and hard count as one floor, and deadly as 5.
  • 🌱 The pets panel shows how much the floor you're on grows them. The egg chance itself hasn't changed: it can drop on any floor.

1.7.7 2026-11-07

Jack's discount on the forge

  • 🔨 Jack's lasting discount (−3% per heart, doubled with marriage or a lifelong friend) now also covers the legendary forge, not just tool upgrades. By mistake it didn't apply there before.

1.7.6 2026-11-07

Village goals without a ceiling

  • 🏘️ Daily village goals: each player can now add up to 1,000 a second instead of 45, so the strongest really race for first place.

1.7.5 2026-11-07

Go to any floor

  • 🔢 In the dungeon, a “Go to floor” button: type the number of any open floor and jump straight there, even floor 1.

1.7.4 2026-11-07

Tool legacy and legendary tools

  • 🧰 With “Tool legacy”, a legendary tool now passes to the new generation as adamantite instead of mithril (legendary counts as a tier above adamantite). The forge itself still starts over, as before.
  • 🔧 If you retired before this update and your tools dropped from adamantite to mithril, they go back to adamantite once.

1.7.3 2026-11-07

Auto-donate

  • 🤝 In the village, under “Donor”: an “Auto-donate every season” button. It pays the Donor for you as soon as each season starts, so hearts never drop and you can forget about them. It stays on even after you retire.

1.7.2 2026-11-07

Light mode for phones

  • 🪶 In settings: “Light mode”. Turns off effects and animations, trims log messages and refreshes the screen more slowly. Handy on phones and in high generations.
  • ⚡ The game is lighter for everyone: the log redraws itself at most about once a second, and the farm no longer rebuilds with every farming level.
  • ⏳ When you come back after being away, the catch-up for the time you were gone is much faster, so the page doesn't freeze when you open it.

1.7.1 2026-11-07

Grace got stronger

  • 👩‍⚕️ Grace's lasting buff grew: on top of +5% max health per heart, she now gives +2% defense per heart. So she really raises your guild power.
  • 🥚 Grace doubles the chance of legendary pet eggs at 10 hearts, and more with marriage or a soul twin.
  • 🛡️ The “food healing” season buff is now “less damage in the dungeon”: up to −10% at 10 hearts, and −30% when married.

1.7 2026-11-07

Legendary pets

  • 🥚 When you clear a dungeon floor, there's a very rare chance of a mystery egg (as rare as the sea kings).
  • 🐣 The egg hatches after 30 minutes and shows what you got: 🐉 a dragon boosts attack, 🦄 a unicorn boosts defense, or 🦅 a griffin boosts power in guild hunts.
  • 🌱 Your pet grows with every floor you clear: baby +10%, teen +25% (after 300 floors), adult +50% (after 1,500 floors).
