The people of Echo Valley

Six people live in Echo Valley, and all of them knew your grandfather. Give each of them something they like once a day. About every two hearts earns you a lasting bonus from that person, up to ten hearts. What they like changes with the season, and someone who gets no gift for half a season becomes upset and loses affection.

When you choose your character you can also marry one of the villagers or keep a lifelong friend, and that choice shapes the family that inherits the farm.

Gifts and hearts, in numbers

Each villager wants two things each season: one they love and one they like. The loved gift gives a full heart and the liked gift gives half a heart. You can give each person one gift per in-game day. A day lasts 150 real seconds and a season is 7 days, so a season is about 17 and a half minutes of play. That means a whole season of daily loved gifts takes someone from zero to 7 hearts.

What each person asks for comes from a fixed kind of item, and the game picks things you have already seen when it can:

  • Emily: crops of the current season.
  • Grace: crops of the season, eggs or milk.
  • Harold: animal products or crops of the season.
  • Salma: animal products.
  • Jack: ores and gems from the mine.
  • James: monster materials from the dungeon.

A golden product counts as the normal one, so golden milk works when someone asks for milk.

The royal gift

Once a season you can also give each person a king fish for +5 hearts at once. Jack and Emily want the Golden carp from the lake, Grace and Salma want the River dragon, and Harold and James want the King of the deep from the sea. If you catch a king fish, do not sell it. It is worth five days of gifts.

When love fades

If you skip someone for 4 in-game days (about 10 minutes of play), they get upset and lose half a heart every day until you give them something. Time away from the game does not count, so closing the game never costs you hearts. Your spouse, your lifelong friend and a soul twin never fade.

Late in the game, the Donor solves this: pay 10 million gold and nobody loses love until the season ends. Turn on the automatic donation and it pays itself at the start of every season, even after you retire.

The seasonal perk

On top of that, each villager has a second perk that changes every season, picked from three. Harold might raise all sell prices, Jack might raise the gem chance, James might shorten hunts. This perk follows hearts in a special way: it is zero at 5 hearts, full at 10, and works against you below 5. At 0 hearts you get the same amount as a penalty. So a villager you ignore can actually slow you down. Look at the line under each card: green means it helps, red means it hurts.

A good first goal is simple: get everyone to 6 hearts so no seasonal perk is negative, then push the two that fit your plan. Emily for a farming generation, Grace and James for a dungeon generation.

Marriage, friends and the soul twin

From generation 5, a villager at 10 hearts can become your spouse (the other gender) or your lifelong friend (the same gender), for 50,000 gold each. Harold is the only one who can be neither. You can have one spouse and one friend at the same time. Each one:

  • counts as 10 extra hearts, so their fixed bonus doubles and their seasonal perk triples;
  • never loses love;
  • adds +10% to all production.

Pick by bonus, not by face. Grace as a bonded partner doubles her health bonus and triples the legendary egg chance. Emily doubles her growth bonus. The Soul twin legacy perk (300 stars) goes further: once you have both a spouse and a friend, one more villager, even Harold, can become your soul twin for 10 million gold. Their bonus becomes ×2.5. Marriage and bonds start over every generation, but the hearts do not quite: villagers start the next life at 2.5 hearts because they remember your family, and the Grandfather's name perk and Grandmother's ring heirloom raise that start. See generations and legacy.

Which buildings first

Most buildings get 3 times more expensive with every level. Coverage for the harvester, planter and sprinklers goes 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, then 100% of the field.

  1. Harvester (300 gold). All five levels cost 36,300 in total. Cheap and it saves the most clicks.
  2. Shipping bin (600). Sells everything that is not locked at the end of each day.
  3. Planter (1,500, all levels 181,500). With the harvester, the field runs by itself.
  4. Inn (2,500) and Kitchen (8,000) once you start the dungeon.
  5. Sprinklers (15,000, all levels 1,815,000). Watering speeds growth, so this pays back on a full field.
  6. Greenhouse (100K, 300K, 1M). Shields 4, 8, then 12 plots from the off-season penalty.

The Village fund is the gold sink for rich families. The first level costs 10 million and each level doubles. Every level adds +1% speed to the farm, barn, mine and lake, forever, for every generation.

The «This week» tab

Since web version 1.24 the weekly content has its own tab, 📅 This week. It has three parts.

The weekly challenge

Every player gets the same challenge, and it changes every Sunday at 00:00 Riyadh time (Saturday 21:00 UTC). The seven challenges take turns: harvest 1,500 crops, collect 1,500 animal products, catch 400 fish, break 800 rocks, defeat 400 monsters, finish 12 guild quests, or earn gold equal to half of your generation's retirement goal. Only what you do after the week starts counts. Finish it and you get +10% gold and XP for 7 days. Retiring does not reset the week, so you can finish it across two generations.

Daily village goals

Three goals are filled by all players together: crops (restarts every day at 06:00 UTC), mine rocks (12:00 UTC) and fish (18:00 UTC). The target follows the village: it is 75% of the last round's total, but never less than 300,000 crops, 150,000 rocks or 80,000 fish. If the village reaches the goal, everyone who helped gets a speed bonus in that area for 7 days, by place: 1st +10%, 2nd +7.5%, 3rd +5%, everyone else +2%. A weaker prize never cuts a stronger one short.

Leaderboards

Six top-10 lists: most gold, deepest dungeon floor, most fish, most harvests and most rocks this week, plus the highest generation. They refresh about every 10 minutes and your numbers go up every 5.

The goals and boards need a linked account, and a save that was edited outside the game cannot take part. The weekly challenge works for everyone.
