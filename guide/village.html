The people of Echo Valley

Six people live in Echo Valley, and all of them knew your grandfather. Give each of them something they like once a day. About every two hearts earns you a lasting bonus from that person, up to ten hearts. What they like changes with the season, and someone who gets no gift for half a season becomes upset and loses affection.

When you choose your character you can also marry one of the villagers or keep a lifelong friend, and that choice shapes the family that inherits the farm.

NameRoleStory
👴 HaroldMayor of Echo ValleyHe has run the village for thirty years and was your grandfather's closest friend. He knows every house and every tale in the valley, and nothing makes him happier than a busy market. Bring him honey and he'll tell you about the old days.
👩‍🌾 EmilyThe village farmerShe grew up among the fields and knows when every seed should go in. She helped your grandfather on the farm when he grew tired, and she hopes to see his land green again. Pumpkins are her favorite thing.
🧔 JackThe village smithHe inherited the forge from his father and says every metal has its own voice. He's a man of few words but never turns anyone away, and bring him silver and he forgets how tired he is and smiles.
👩‍⚕️ GraceThe village doctorShe came back to the valley after years of study in the city and opened a small clinic by the square. She patches up mercenaries returning from the dungeon, and says strawberries are the best medicine for the heart.
🤺 JamesLeader of the mercenary guildHe roamed distant lands as a mercenary before settling in the valley and founding the guild. He's hard on newcomers because he lost friends in the depths, but he respects anyone who proves themselves. He keeps monster fangs as souvenirs of his battles.
👩‍🦱 SalmaLivestock breederShe raised generations of animals on her family's farm beyond the hills, and can tell at a glance which two will give the best young. She came to the valley when she heard about the breeding houses. She loves wool and milk, and dreams of seeing the river dragon with her own eyes.
