UntoldLife

You inherit your grandfather's farm in Echo Valley. You start with a small field and a bag of turnip seeds, and build a whole valley from it: a barn, a mine, a lake, an adventurers' guild, a smith and a village of people who know your family.

Your farmer grows old, and when the time comes hands the farm to their child. Each generation starts stronger than the last, and after ten generations the Industrial Age opens: a factory, services for the village, and the First Train.

  • 24 crops, 4 seasons
  • Generations and legacy
  • A mine and a dungeon
  • A factory and a train
  • Keeps going while you are away

What is new in UntoldLife

We update the game constantly, and most changes begin as a message from a player.

1.22.3 · 2026-11-14

A quicker way in

🚪 A lighter first screen: the “Enter the village” button now comes right after the letter, and your name, character and language sit below it as options. Plus a loading screen with the logo instead of an empty page on a slow connection.

1.22.2 · 2026-11-13

Notifications while you're away

🔔 Optional notifications under “More”: you get one when a ship is back, a contract is ready, the factory stops, or you can retire, while you're in another window. They work as long as the game is open in the background.

1.22.1 · 2026-11-12

Install the game as an app

📲 An “Install as an app” button under “More”: the game sits on your home screen or desktop and opens in its own window. On iPhone it tells you how, from the Share button.

About us

A small studio doing what it loves

369Games is a small independent studio. We have no publisher and no investors; we have games we love making and players we listen to.

We design, code and test our games ourselves. We began with a farming game because we love the genre, and we will try other kinds with each new game. The goal is simple: games we are proud of, that respect the time of the people who play them.

More about us
We play what we makeEvery feature is tried by us before it reaches you, and if it is not fun it does not ship.
Players are partnersWe read every message, and much of what is in our games today began as a player's suggestion.
Straight with youEvery change is written in the update log, and we say openly how we make our games and which tools we use.
